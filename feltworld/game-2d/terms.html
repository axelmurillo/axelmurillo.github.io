Terms of use

This is a free, fictional storybook game from the Feltworld project. There is no account, purchase, subscription or prize.

Playing the story

Your choices affect the fictional world. The story is entertainment, not psychological, medical or professional advice. You can stop at any time. Keyboard, sound, colour and motion controls are available in Settings.

Your saved adventure

The game can remember your choices in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data, switching browsers or playing without saving can remove that progress. “Your choices” shows an automatic record of what you selected; it is not a diary you need to write.
