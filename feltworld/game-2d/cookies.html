Cookies & storage

The game itself sets no cookies. It uses browser local storage to remember this adventure and the settings you select. It does not use that storage for analytics, advertising or tracking you across sites.

What is stored

Local-storage keyPurposeWhen it ends
little-things-adventure-v1Your story actions and glimmers, used to restore progress and “Your choices”.Until you switch off saving, replace the adventure or clear site data.
little-things-settings-v1Your selected theme, short-effect volume, motion setting and Nomie’s fringe colour.Until you switch off remembering preferences or clear site data.
little-things-storage-v1Only your choices about whether saving and preference storage are enabled.Until you change those controls or clear site data. This small record remembers an objection so other data is not read or saved on your next visit.

Choose what to remember

Open storage settings →

Switch off “Save this adventure” to delete the stored copy and continue in this tab without saving. Switch off “Remember my preferences” to delete the stored settings and keep your current settings only for this tab. Reloading or closing the tab then starts from the defaults. Your choice to switch storage off is remembered, so you do not need to object again on every visit.

Why there is no “accept all” banner

The game saves only actions needed to restore the adventure you request and preferences that change how it appears or functions. The current ICO guidance provides narrowly defined exceptions for these purposes; preference storage also requires clear information and a simple, free way to object. The controls above provide that choice. We are not treating all local storage as automatically exempt.

If analytics, ads, embedded third-party services or other storage are added, their purposes and any consent requirements must be assessed separately before they run.

Browser caching

Your browser may cache images, scripts and fonts as part of displaying the game. Fonts, images and code are served with the game; no remote font or media service is embedded. Your browser’s own settings control its cache.
